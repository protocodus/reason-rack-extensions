## Product details

YouKnow puts a direct polysynth signal path into the Reason rack. Mix saw,
pulse, sub, and noise, shape them with a resonant filter and shared envelope,
and add Chorus I, II, or the dedicated narrow I+II combination. Velocity,
glide, key modes, and up to 16 voices support expressive playing.

Explore 93 original Protocodus patches with balanced browsing levels, then
make them your own on a readable 8RU panel. The rear Unit Character control
varies circuit tolerances and saves with your patch. Aging models service
drift and noise changes, starts at 50%, and saves with the song. Set Aging to
0% for the freshly serviced state; existing songs recall their stored value.

Eight rear CV inputs connect YouKnow to the rack: Note and Gate play a
monophonic part, Cutoff and Resonance add modulation to their panel values,
and Volume, Amp, Sub, and Noise scale their respective settings. MIDI remains
polyphonic. All 41 sound, performance, and quality controls support sequencer
automation and Remote mapping, alongside standard wheels and sustain.

Four rear processing controls offer 1x/2x/4x quality, three saturation modes,
two early interpolation choices, and three filter solvers. The efficient
default is 1x, Poly, Cubic, and Normal. These settings stay with the song;
Quality changes wait for quiet voices and tails before a short fade. Front
status readouts keep the rear settings visible while you play.

Highlights:

- Circuit-modelled oscillator, resonant filter, VCA, envelope, and stereo BBD
  chorus behavior
- 93 original patches with Reason browser categories and tags
- Up to 16-voice MIDI, eight rear CV inputs, and stereo auto-routing
- 41 automatable custom controls plus standard performance controllers
- Fixed 41-sample latency across every Quality setting
- Rear processing-quality and unit-model controls with front status readouts

The 16-voice/4x combination has the highest CPU cost. Choose processing quality
and polyphony to suit the rest of your rack. YouKnow requires Reason 14 or
later and Rack Extension SDK 5 support.
